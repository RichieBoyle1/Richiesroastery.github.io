Here is our story

Our journey began during the challenges of the pandemic when our founder, Richie, faced redundancy and decided to turn that adversity into an opportunity and follow her passion for coffee. Richie first delved into the world of specialty coffee while working in Melbourne coffee shops back in 2011 and dreamt of having her own little spot for the 10 years that followed.

Our coffee shop by the river is not just a place to grab your daily caffeine fix; it's a destination where the love for quality coffee blends seamlessly with the beauty of our surroundings. The calming flow of the river, the inviting aroma of freshly brewed coffee and the warm ambiance of our space create an atmosphere where you can unwind, socialize, or simply enjoy a moment of solitude.
